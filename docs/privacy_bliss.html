Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 21, 2026

App: Bliss — iOS

Introduction

Bliss ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Bliss iOS application (the "App").

Bliss is designed as a self-discovery and reflective experience. We intentionally minimize data usage and do not collect personally identifiable information.

Information We Collect

Bliss does not require account creation and does not collect personal data.

  • Usernames: A username chosen by the user is stored locally on the device only and is not linked to any real identity.
  • App Inputs: Character selections and in-app responses are processed to generate personalized narratives but are not used to identify users.
  • Device Data: Bliss does not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or precise location data.
  • Usage Data: We do not collect analytics, tracking data, or behavioral logs.
Bliss does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or social media data.

How Information Is Used

Any information processed within the App is used solely to:

  • Generate personalized psychological narratives and archetype insights.
  • Provide relationship and character-based reflections.
  • Operate the core features of the App locally or temporarily during processing.

We do not use information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

AI and Psychological Analysis

Bliss uses AI-powered analysis inspired by Jungian psychology to generate reflective stories and archetype insights.

This analysis is not medical, diagnostic, or therapeutic in nature and should not be considered professional mental health advice.

Third-Party Services

Bliss does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Apple system services (such as iOS runtime services) may process limited technical information as required for normal app operation, subject to Apple’s own privacy policies.

Sharing and Disclosure

Bliss does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

No user-generated content is publicly visible or shared with other users.

Data Retention

Bliss does not maintain user accounts or persistent personal records. Data created during app usage is retained only as necessary for the app experience and may be cleared when the app is deleted.

Your Rights

Because Bliss does not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete.

If you have questions about privacy, you may contact us using the details below.

Security

We follow reasonable security practices appropriate to the minimal data handled by the App. However, no application can be completely secure, and users are encouraged to keep their devices updated and protected.

Children

Bliss is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
