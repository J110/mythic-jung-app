# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 18, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to Mythic Jung ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Username**: A unique identifier you choose to access the app
- **Character Preferences**: Fictional characters you select that resonate with you
- **Relationship Information**: Optional data about your relationships if you use the relationship features

### Information Automatically Collected
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app, features used
- **Error Logs**: Crash reports and error information for improving the app

### Information from Third Parties
- **AI Processing**: We use OpenAI's services to generate personalized psychological insights. Your character selections are processed by OpenAI to generate content. See OpenAI's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide personalized psychological insights and narratives
- Generate archetype analysis and relationship compatibility
- Improve our app and services
- Respond to your requests or questions
- Ensure the security and integrity of our services

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely on our servers
- We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties
- We retain your data as long as you have an active account

## Data Sharing

We may share your information with:
- **Service Providers**: Third-party services that help us operate the app (hosting, AI processing)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger or acquisition

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and data
- **Data Portability**: Request your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@mythicjung.com

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.
